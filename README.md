# HR Attrition Analysis (Excel)

An Excel dashboard analysing employee attrition on a public sample dataset of 1,470 employees (IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance, from Kaggle). The data is fictional, so this is a skills demonstration, not a real company's results.

**Files**
- HR_Attrition_Dashboard.pdf: one-page dashboard
- HR_Attrition_Analysis.xlsx: full workbook (data, pivot tables, charts)

**Tools used:** Excel Tables, helper columns (attrition flag, age band, tenure band), pivot tables, COUNTIFS/AVERAGEIFS, charts

**Key findings**
- Employees working overtime left at 30.5%, versus 10.4% for those who did not.
- Employees in their first two years left at 29.8%, versus 8.1% for those with 10+ years.
- Sales Representatives had the highest attrition (39.8%); Research Directors the lowest (2.5%).

**Recommendations**
- Review workload and overtime in high-attrition roles.
- Prioritise onboarding and early-career retention conversations.

These are associations in a sample dataset, not proof of cause.

**Dataset:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance (Kaggle)
